Legal & Translation Notice

Important notices regarding AI translations and non-hosting policies.

AI Translation Notice

The subtitles cataloged on ls-subtitle are translated from source languages using Artificial Intelligence (LLM) models and formatted for community viewing.

  • Accuracy: While modern neural translation models yield high quality, translations may occasionally contain localized phrasing nuances or literal interpretations of idioms.
  • Timing & Sync: Subtitles are aligned to specific release rips. If playing a different video version, you can adjust subtitle sync in media players like VLC using the keyboard shortcuts G (delay) and H (hasten).

Non-Hosting & Copyright Policy

ls-subtitle operates strictly as a text index:

  • No Video / Audio Hosting: This project does not host, stream, link to, or store any copyrighted video or audio streams under any circumstances.
  • Plain-Text Only: All files hosted in the GitHub repository consist exclusively of text subtitles (.srt) and zip archives (.zip) containing subtitle files.
  • Trademarks: Title names, characters, and franchise brands are the property of their respective copyright owners and are cited solely for descriptive and indexing purposes.
